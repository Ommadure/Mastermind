# Zero-Cost Tier-1 Source Curation Standard & Directory

This reference governs **Stage 4 (Zero-Cost Tier-1 Curriculum Architecture)** of the `mastermind` skill. Every learning roadmap must be **100% free of cost** and route the user exclusively to primary specifications, open university curricula, peer-reviewed research, or thoroughly vetted open-source textbooks and sandboxes.

## 1. The 3-Tier Source Verification Hierarchy

Never recommend paid courses, paywalled certificates, affiliate listicles, or shallow SEO blog posts. Rank and select free resources strictly in this order:

### Tier 1A: Canonical Primary Sources & Official Specifications (Gold Standard)
- Official language, compiler, kernel, or framework documentation written by the core maintainers.
- Formal technical standards and specifications (IETF RFCs, W3C Recommendations, IEEE/NIST open standards, ISO drafts, hardware datasheets).
- Primary statutory, regulatory, clinical, or financial filings (SEC EDGAR, Federal Reserve FRED, WHO/CDC clinical guidelines).

### Tier 1B: Open University Curricula & Peer-Reviewed Literature
- Full, unpaywalled university courses containing lecture videos/notes, reading lists, problem sets, and exams.
- Seminal and state-of-the-art peer-reviewed papers hosted on open-access repositories.

### Tier 1C: Community-Reviewed Open Textbooks & Interactive Practice Sandboxes
- Open-access textbooks reviewed by thousands of practitioners or university faculty.
- Free browser-based or open-source interactive problem-solving, simulation, and coding platforms.

## 3. Link Hygiene & Citation Rules

When presenting a Zero-Cost Mastery Roadmap to the user:
1. **Run a Targeted Web Search First**: For any specialized skill not covered in the table above, search specifically for open university syllabi, official documentation, or open-access textbooks on that exact topic.
2. **Deep-Link to Specific Modules**: Link directly to the specific documentation guide, book chapter, or course syllabus rather than a generic domain root whenever available.
3. **Explain the Pedagogical Why**: Beside each recommended free link, state in one crisp phrase *why* it was chosen (e.g., *"Authoritative maintainer reference for ownership semantics"* or *"Includes 12 graded lab assignments with starter code"*) and *which chapters/sections to skip* to preserve 80/20 efficiency.
